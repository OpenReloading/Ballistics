# Practical-shooting target mark

## Question

Reference data, target faces: may a face drawn from the dimensions that the IPSC Handgun Competition Rules state for their paper targets ship after the current pre-release milestone, without the body's name or logo as a mark, and under which conditions? This is not legal advice.

## Method

Primary sources only, read on 2026-10-30:

- Trade mark registers: a name search in TMview, the register search of the EU intellectual property office that combines the EUIPO, DPMA, USPTO, WIPO (Madrid) and other national registers (https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/), for "IPSC", "IPSC TARGET", "IPSC TARGETS" and "USPSA". Every hit by the body or its US region was opened in its register record. The register entries: EUIPO eSearch (https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/#details/trademarks/018980378), USPTO TSDR (https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=98007111&caseType=SERIAL_NO&searchType=statusSearch), DPMAregister (https://register.dpma.de/).
- The body's terms: the trade mark notice (https://www.ipsc.org/trademark/), the copyright and trade mark lines on the cover of the Handgun Competition Rules, January 2026 edition (rule books page https://www.ipsc.org/ipsc-rules/rule-books/, the English handgun PDF linked there, 81 pages, SHA-256 `1ba68a56d5d84158c38a0bad9d0935f1ac035e8a4dd3aaaa2301f66576884668`), its Appendix B2 (printed page 49), and the brand book (https://ipsc.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Brandbook-IPSC-Copyright-2022.pdf).
- Statutes: Regulation (EU) 2017/1001, Articles 9 and 14 (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32017R1001); MarkenG § 23 (https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/markeng/__23.html); UrhG § 2 (https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__2.html); 15 U.S.C. 1115(b)(4) (https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title15/html/USCODE-2023-title15-chap22-subchapIII-sec1115.htm), 15 U.S.C. 1125(c)(3) (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1125), 17 U.S.C. 102(b) (https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title17/html/USCODE-2023-title17-chap1-sec102.htm).

Case law was not read. Every point that rests on it is marked unverified. Nothing from the rule book's text or drawings is copied here; no outline is built.

## Formulas

None: a legal research question, no model equation. The tests applied, in our own words:

- EU and Germany: the owner of a mark cannot stop a third party in the course of trade from using a sign to state a characteristic of its own goods (kind, intended purpose), or from using the mark to identify or refer to the owner's goods or services, as long as the use follows honest practices (Regulation (EU) 2017/1001, Article 14(1)(b), (c) and (2); MarkenG § 23(1) Nr. 2 and 3 and (2)).
- US: using a term otherwise than as a mark, fairly and in good faith, only to describe one's own goods is a defence (15 U.S.C. 1115(b)(4)). The statute names nominative fair use only as an exclusion from dilution (15 U.S.C. 1125(c)(3)(A)); for infringement it is court doctrine (unverified here).
- Copyright: in the US no protection extends to a system, method or fact, whatever form describes it (17 U.S.C. 102(b)). In Germany technical depictions such as drawings are works when they are a personal intellectual creation (UrhG § 2(1) Nr. 7 and (2)); how low that bar sits for technical drawings, and that the protection covers the drawing and not the shape it shows, is case law (unverified here).

## Numbers

Answers to the five questions of the issue.

1. Register entries.
   - EU trade mark 018980378 "IPSC", word mark, owner International Practical Shooting Confederation, filed 2024-01-30, registered 2024-05-29, expires 2034-01-30. Nice classes 13 (firearms, ammunition), 16 (printed matter, manuals), 25 (clothing), 28 (sporting articles, including targets for firearms and electronic targets), 35 and 41 (sport and training services, publishing). No class 9 (software) and no class 42. It covers Germany.
   - US: no live registration of "IPSC" owned by the body was found. The "IPSC" entries in the USPTO register are ended or belong to unrelated owners.
   - US region: "USPSA", word mark, US registration 7647645 (serial 98007111), owner United States Practical Shooting Association, registered 2025-01-14, class 28 firearm targets and class 35. Also "USPSA DVC" (registration 8005435, figurative, a diamond with lettering, class 35) and a published application "USPSA" (serial 99751585, classes 9, 16 and 41, including downloadable rule books).
   - Regional bodies hold their own marks (for example Benelux 1250335 and 1250355, Austria 903/1999 and 10468/2019). They do not change the answer.
   - The rule book's cover lists "IPSC Targets" among the body's registered marks. A name search found no register entry for "IPSC TARGET" or "IPSC TARGETS" in any office TMview covers: claimed by the body, not found in a register. The shield logo shown in the brand book holds a shield, star, globe and lettering, no target figure. Whether any figurative or shape mark without a word element shows the target is unverified: the owner-name filter of the register search could not be used, and a figure search was not run.
2. The body's terms. The trade mark notice names the IPSC, IROA, MISIA, IPSCWorld and ESS names, logos, emblems and designs as the body's property and bars commercial use, reproduction, modification or display of them without its prior written consent; it says nothing about descriptive or nominative use, about target drawings or about third-party software. The rule book cover claims copyright in the book, lists the marks (including "IPSC Targets") and bars their use by parties not affiliated with the body without written approval of its president. The brand book's disclaimer covers the emblem only. No term grants a licence to the rule book text or drawings. Whether these site and cover terms bind a reader who never agreed to them is a point for a lawyer.
3. Nominative or descriptive use. A statement such as "scores the target of the IPSC Handgun Competition Rules, January 2026 edition, Appendix B2", with no logo, no claim of approval and no copy of the drawing, refers to the body's rule book as its own and fits Article 14(1)(c) of the EU regulation and MarkenG § 23(1) Nr. 3 (medium confidence, statute read, no case law). Naming the face itself "IPSC Target" in a printed or downloaded target is weaker: class 28 of the EU mark covers firearm targets, so a printable face sold or given away under that name is the kind of goods the mark covers. In the US the body holds no live registration found here; for the "USPSA" mark the statutory defence is descriptive fair use only, and nominative fair use against infringement is court doctrine (unverified).
4. Copyright. Appendix B2 states the outer size (45 by 57 cm), the border (0.5 cm) and most zone corners as dimension lines measured from the left and top edges. A few points (the top edge of the A zone, where the C zone meets the top of the border, and symmetry between the top and bottom edges) are shown only as drawn, not dimensioned. Rebuilding the outline from the stated numbers takes facts, which 17 U.S.C. 102(b) leaves free in the US, and in Germany UrhG § 2 protects the drawing, not the dimensions (low confidence for Germany, case law unread). The repository must not copy or embed the appendix image, trace it, or reproduce its layout of dimension lines or labels. A point the appendix does not dimension is either derived from stated numbers in the test, or marked as an assumption with a note to check a physical target.
5. Answer for the owner: safe only with conditions (medium confidence for the EU and Germany, low for the US), see Result.

## Result

no change. Documented as a limit for later faces, not adopted in code; no face is built and no build issue passes the "Worth building?" gate (the faces depend on the competition-event work of issue #221 and issue #222, which already holds them).

Conditions under which a face drawn from stated dimensions may ship after the current pre-release milestone:

- The face has a neutral name of its own (for example "Practical-shooting target, octagon, 45 x 57 cm"). "IPSC" or "USPSA" appears only in the citation of the provenance and on the page that explains the source, as a reference to the rule book, edition and appendix.
- No logo, no shield, no motto, no "official", "approved" or "IPSC target" claim; one line that the project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the body.
- A printed or downloaded face carries neither mark as a name. Printed pages already carry no notice (`AGENTS.md`), so the neutral name is the safeguard there.
- No copy, trace or embed of a rule book diagram; every vertex from a stated dimension or an assumption marked as such; a test recomputes the zones.
- The owner asks a lawyer before shipping to confirm: whether a free open-source project acts in the course of trade at all; whether a printable target face counts as goods of class 28 or as goods similar to them under Article 9(2)(b) of the EU regulation, or calls on a reputation under Article 9(2)(c); whether the body's site and cover terms bind; the German threshold for technical drawings; and the US position on the "USPSA" mark.

## Confidence
