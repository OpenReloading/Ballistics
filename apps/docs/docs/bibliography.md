# Bibliography

Measurement data imported as values carry their license: public domain in the US for reports written by US Government employees and approved for unlimited release (17 U.S.C. 105). Dimensions from the C.I.P. sheets and the rule books are recorded as facts. Every other source is cited only. No text, table or figure of a source is copied.
