usage: ballistics compare --rows <path> --baseline <m3|module|verify.json> --candidate <m3|module> [options]

options:
  --rows <path>                       a rows file, a folder of .csv files or -
                                      for stdin (default: BALLISTICS_ROWS);
                                      catalogue for the published loads of a
                                      first --records root with every kind
                                      directory, or a folder laid out as
                                      records/loads/ (maker tables only)
  --baseline <m3|module|verify.json>  m3, a module or a saved verify result
  --candidate <m3|module>             m3 or a module
  --records <dir>                     a records root, repeatable (default:
                                      BALLISTICS_RECORDS, roots joined by : or
                                      on Windows ;); a first root with every
                                      kind directory, as the records folder of a
                                      repository clone, replaces the catalog
                                      bundle, other roots add their records to
                                      it; the sources an added record cites must
                                      be records of the adding roots
  --split <name>                      the split (default: validation) (choices:
                                      "test", "validation", "training")
  --final                             required for the test split
  --group-by <key>                    also group by powder, family, cartridge,
                                      bullet or construction
  --params <params>                   the powder parameters of the records
                                      (default), or the burn coefficients fitted
                                      to the training rows (choices: "records",
                                      "fit-on-training")
  --fit <fit.json>                    instead of --params: evaluate the values
                                      of a saved fit of the same rows and
                                      records without fitting; the result is
                                      marked as a screen with this build version
                                      and carries the fit model version
  --velocity-sigma <fraction>         fit and --params fit-on-training: velocity
                                      sigma of a row, a fraction of its velocity
                                      (default 0.03)
  --exponent <exponent>               fit and --params fit-on-training: choose
                                      the pressure exponent per powder family
                                      and fit each burn coefficient at it
                                      (choices: "family")
  --family <fit.json>                 with --exponent family: take the family
                                      choice of a saved fit of the same rows,
                                      records, velocity sigma and build instead
                                      of making it
  --pressure-sigma <fraction>         fit and --params fit-on-training: fit the
                                      printed pressures too, with this sigma, a
                                      fraction of the pressure, and one factor
                                      per key
  --pressure-factor <key>             with --pressure-sigma: the key of a
                                      pressure factor, basis (the row's pressure
                                      basis, default), table, cartridge or
                                      table-cartridge (both)
  --energy <energy>                   fit and --params fit-on-training: prior,
                                      each powder's energy is its prior from
                                      thermochemistry times one energy-level
                                      factor chosen on the training pressures
                                      and only the burn coefficient is fitted
                                      per powder; family, each powder's energy
                                      is fitted with its burn coefficient, under
                                      a prior on its family's median impetus
                                      (all reference powders' for a family with
                                      fewer than 5) that tightens as its used
                                      rows (below 20) or cartridges (below 4)
                                      fall (choices: "prior", "family")
  --json                              output format JSON (default: JSON unless
                                      stdout is a terminal)
  --table                             output format a table (default on a
                                      terminal)
  -h, --help                          this text
