usage: ballistics fit --rows <path> [--model m3|<module>] [options]

options:
  --rows <path>                a rows file, a folder of .csv files or - for
                               stdin (default: BALLISTICS_ROWS); catalogue for
                               the published loads of a first --records root
                               with every kind directory, or a folder laid out
                               as records/loads/ (maker tables only)
  --model <m3|module>          m3 (default), the model of the installed
                               @rangefoundry/ballistics, or a module that
                               exports interior(load, options?)
  --records <dir>              a records root, repeatable (default:
                               BALLISTICS_RECORDS, roots joined by : or on
                               Windows ;); a first root with every kind
                               directory, as the records folder of a repository
                               clone, replaces the catalog bundle, other roots
                               add their records to it; the sources an added
                               record cites must be records of the adding roots
  --velocity-sigma <fraction>  fit and --params fit-on-training: velocity sigma
                               of a row, a fraction of its velocity (default
                               0.03)
  --exponent <exponent>        fit and --params fit-on-training: choose the
                               pressure exponent per powder family and fit each
                               burn coefficient at it (choices: "family")
  --family <fit.json>          with --exponent family: take the family choice of
                               a saved fit of the same rows, records, velocity
                               sigma and build instead of making it
  --pressure-sigma <fraction>  fit and --params fit-on-training: fit the printed
                               pressures too, with this sigma, a fraction of the
                               pressure, and one factor per key
  --pressure-factor <key>      with --pressure-sigma: the key of a pressure
                               factor, basis (the row's pressure basis,
                               default), table, cartridge or table-cartridge
                               (both)
  --energy <energy>            fit and --params fit-on-training: prior, each
                               powder's energy is its prior from thermochemistry
                               times one energy-level factor chosen on the
                               training pressures and only the burn coefficient
                               is fitted per powder; family, each powder's
                               energy is fitted with its burn coefficient, under
                               a prior on its family's median impetus (all
                               reference powders' for a family with fewer than
                               5) that tightens as its used rows (below 20) or
                               cartridges (below 4) fall (choices: "prior",
                               "family")
  --folds                      cross-validate over 5 folds of the cartridges of
                               the training and validation splits (the fold of a
                               cartridge is its place, mod 5, in the order of
                               the SHA-256 of 2026-11-14/<cartridge id>); each
                               fold is scored by a fit, with the fit options, on
                               the other four, and the result holds each fold
                               and all folds together; the test split is not
                               read; verify takes no --split, --params or --fit
                               with it
  --json                       output format JSON (default: JSON unless stdout
                               is a terminal)
  --table                      output format a table (default on a terminal)
  -h, --help                   this text

exit codes: 0 done, 1 refused run, 64 bad command line, 65 bad data, 66 missing or
unreadable input, 70 internal error
