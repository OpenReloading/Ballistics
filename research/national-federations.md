# National shooting federations outside Germany with a public rule book

Question (issue 332): which national federations outside Germany publish a rule book for scored target disciplines, and which are worth records.
Retrieval date for every URL below: 2026-10-27. Rule books are described in own words; nothing is quoted.

## Findings in short

- The ISSF lists 163 member federations from 149 countries or territories (the page itself shows about 160 entries). Almost every member federation that publishes rules does so as a translation of the ISSF rule book plus a short national layer. For those, a record adds the federation entry and its national disciplines, but few new target faces.
- New faces are worth drawing only where the federation defines its own: the NRA of America (US), the NSRA (UK), the NBRSA (US benchrest), the F-Class rules of ICFRA, and one national 100 m face of the Austrian federation.
- Every federation on the ranked list publishes its rule book as a free download without login (USA Shooting: partly Word files). None states an open licence; the notices are copyright footers. The one explicit permission is Finland's: the SAL rules page says the online versions may be copied and printed, which is not an open licence. Reuse of text therefore stays "not granted"; rules and dimensions would be re-expressed in own words, as for the existing records.
- Observation for the ISSF record: the US and Canadian federation pages and a search result on the ISSF back office point to a 2026 ISSF rule book effective 1 July 2026 (rifle and pistol: live aiming changes). Today's ISSF record cites the 2025 third print. I did not open the 2026 book, so its edition data is unverified.

### 1. NRA of America

- Site: https://www.nra.org ; rule books: https://competitions.nra.org (retrieved as PDFs).
- High Power Rifle Rules, revised February 2025, 100 pages: https://competitions.nra.org/media/10025/2025-high_power_rifle_rules.pdf . Section 4 defines 13 target designations for 100 to 1000 yards (SR-1, SR-21, MR-31, SR, SR-42, MR-52, SR-5, SR-3, MR-63, MR-65, MR-1, LR, LR Center). Section 7 lists 13 numbered individual courses (7.1 to 7.13) plus team and special courses (not counted). Section 24 covers 300 m.
- Smallbore Rifle Rules, revised January 2023: https://competitions.nra.org/media/8920/2023-smallbore-rifle-rules.pdf . Section 4 defines 14 target sections (4.2 to 4.15, several with sub-variants) for 50 ft to 200 yd. It states which NRA faces are scaled copies of the ISSF 50 m and 300 m faces (4.8, 4.10, 4.12), so the ISSF link is partly named. Conventional and metric prone and position courses are in section 7.
- F-Class Rules, revised January 2024: https://competitions.nra.org/media/9392/2024-f-class-rulebook.pdf . F-Class centres are pasted on MR-63, MR-65, MR-1 and LR faces (section 4); they change the X-ring scoring.
- Also published, not read (unverified): International Rifle Rules, Rifle Silhouette Rules (hit or miss steel, no scored rings).
- Reuse: the High Power and F-Class books carry a 2020 NRA copyright line; no licence text found.

### 2. NSRA (UK)

- Site: https://www.nsra.co.uk ; rules page: https://www.nsra.co.uk/rules-information ; book: https://www.nsra.co.uk/s/Rules-Regs-2024.pdf (also a version with changes marked).
- One book for small-bore and air rifle, small-bore and air pistol, and crossbow. Air rifle, air pistol and running target defer to the ISSF rule book (sold by the NSRA), so those disciplines map to ISSF disciplines by statement.
- Appendix A holds ring-dimension tables for about 20 face types (long range 1989 series, decimal cadet series, metric, skirmisher, six pistol faces, air gun faces, ISSF faces by reference to the ISSF technical rules). Exact count not taken.
- Also on the page: benchrest clarification and benchrest target explanation, scoring guidance (2012). Copyright footer "NSRA 2007 to 2024"; no licence.
- The UK federation in the ISSF list is British Shooting (https://www.britishshooting.org.uk), an elite body; I did not check it for a rule book (unverified).

### 3. Swiss Shooting (SSV)

- Site: https://www.swissshooting.ch ; rule page: https://www.swissshooting.ch/de/schiesssport/breitensport/dokumente-und-reglemente/
- Rules for sport shooting (RSpS), edition 2022 (revision date 9 Jul 2021), nine parts: https://www.swissshooting.ch/media/0xikjm12/dok-1104020_4029-d.pdf . Rifle part (TRG): https://www.swissshooting.ch/media/sslercxt/dok-1104022-d.pdf ; pistol part (TRP): https://www.swissshooting.ch/media/g1uft33k/dok-1104023-d.pdf .
- TRG articles 25 to 27 set face sizes for 10 m, 50 m and 300 m rifle and name four scoring variants (face 5, 10, 20, 100) with ring and black diameters. Pistol faces are not defined: TRP refers to the federal shooting ordinance and the ISSF. The federal ordinance was not read.
- Notable content: 300 m ordnance rifle and ordnance pistol classes, supported-position (Auflage) rules, ISSF correspondence stated per discipline in TRG and TRP tables.
- Language: German only on the page. The RSpS says the German text prevails over other language versions, so French or Italian versions probably exist; none found (unverified). No reuse statement on the page; the legal notice pages were not read.

### 4. KNSA (NL)

- Site: https://www.knsa.nl ; rules: https://www.knsa.nl/de-knsa/wet-en-regelgeving/schiet-en-wedstrijdreglementen/knsa/ (ten parts, one PDF each, plus one merged PDF).
- Read: part III small-bore rifle (version 1 Oct 2026), part VI pistol (13 Oct 2026), part II air rifle (1 Oct 2024). All three say the ISSF rules apply wherever the national text is silent, and have a chapter on ranges and faces. Part IV large-bore rifle is dated 1 Oct 2024 (title seen, not read).
- The page names separate rule books for recognised disciplines such as silhouette, benchrest, field target (taken from a search snippet of the "recognised disciplines" page, not opened: unverified).
- The site's certificate chain did not verify; the pages and PDFs were read read-only with verification off.

### 5. ÖSB (AT)

- Site: https://www.schuetzenbund.at ; rule page: https://schuetzenbund.at/sparten-ligen/gewehr/regelwerke-richtlinien/
- Austrian shooting order ÖSchO, first edition revision 15, valid from 1 Sep 2025, copyright ÖSB: https://www.schuetzenbund.at/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/OeSchO_1_Ausgabe_Revision_15_.pdf . Lists competitions per age class for rifle, pistol, running target and large-calibre pistol, with times; faces are the internationally approved ones except one national 100 m face (350 x 395 mm, rings 1 to 10 plus inner ten).
- Partial German translations of the ISSF rules are on the page (old version for national events, 2026 version for international ones); the page says the English original governs.
- The ISSF list shows a second Austrian member, Austria Sportschützen Fachverband (http://www.asf-shooting.at); not checked (unverified).

### 6. FFTir (FR)

- Site: https://www.fftir.org ; rule list: https://www.fftir.org/reglements-sportifs/
- Eight ISSF translations (technical, rifle, pistol, running target, shotgun, five-shot air pistol, paper target annex). The paper target annex is the 2025 ISSF edition effective 1 Jan 2026, updated by the French referee committee on 28 Feb 2026: https://www.fftir.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Reglement_ISSF_2025_Annexe-Regles-Cibles-Papier_diffusion.pdf . It states that articles not applicable in France are handled by separate notes on the site (not read).
- National rule books by season: service-weapon shooting (TAR, 2025-2026), ISR and mini ISR, muzzle-loading (MLAIC), shooting schools. French versions of benchrest (WBSF, 2017), rimfire (WBSF 2025, WRABF 2026) and metallic silhouette (IMSSU 2025).
- The page has no separate national rules for 10 m, 50 m, 25 m or 300 m; those run on the ISSF translations. Copyright footer "FFTir 2012 to 2026"; ISSF text keeps ISSF copyright.

### 7. NBRSA (US benchrest)

- Site: https://www.nbrsa.org ; rules: https://www.nbrsa.org/rules-forms/ . Short range rules and by-laws (rules rev. 48 January 2026, by-laws rev. 44 December 2025): https://www.nbrsa.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/rule_book_nbrsa_short_range.pages.pdf . Long range (600 and 1000 yard) book: https://www.nbrsa.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/rule_book_nbrsa_long_range.pages.pdf .
- Targets: BR-100, BR-200, BR-300 card layouts, group and score shooting sections, a target drawing appendix. Copyright 2026 NBRSA; no licence.
- Other benchrest and rimfire bodies (IBS, WBSF) not read.

### 8. Finland, 9. Sweden, 10. Norway

- Finland (SAL): https://www.ampumaurheiluliitto.fi/kilpailutoiminta/kilpailusaannot/ . Rifle rules K/2026-2 (300 m, 300 m standard rifle, 50 m, 10 m air, a national three-position age class): https://www.ampumaurheiluliitto.fi/content/uploads/2026/08/Kivaarisaannot-2026-2.pdf ; also pistol rules 2026, PRS rules 2025, silhouette 2025, black powder 2023. The rifle book says it follows the ISSF competition rules. Only Finnish found.
- Sweden (SvSF): https://www.skyttesport.se/taevlingsverksamhet/reglementen/ . Swedish translations of ISSF chapters 6 to 10 (valid from 19 Oct 2026 per page), national additions for national events, national mixed-team 25 m and 50 m standard pistol rules (Nov 2022). Pages for Nordic rifle events and long-range events exist; not opened (unverified).
- Norway (NSF): https://www.skyting.no . General technical rules 2026 (Norwegian ISSF translation with an annex on judging paper targets): https://www.skyting.no/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/gtr-2026-v3.pdf ; national pistol rules (rapid, NAIS, PPC 1500, field pistol, T96) exist as PDFs from the same site (titles seen in search, not read: unverified).
- None of the three states a reuse licence.

### 11. ICFRA F-Class

- International body, not national: https://nra.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/ICFRA-Rules-for-F-Class.pdf (host: the UK NRA). 2023 edition; the approval line in the copy is blank. Annex B defines the F-Class target for short and long range (B2, B3) and electronic target specification. Rifle classes Open and F/TR, prone only.
- I did not check ICFRA's own site for a newer copy. The UK NRA's current fullbore rule book for Imperial Target Rifle was not confirmed (only a 2017 archive copy and the handbooks for gallery rifle and pistol, 2025, were seen in search results, not opened: unverified).

### 12. USA Shooting

- https://www.usashooting.org/7-events/usasrules lists general technical rules (2013), rifle and pistol rules (2011, Word files), and links to the ISSF rule book. These are ISSF adaptations and add few new faces. General technical rules: https://usashooting.org/app/uploads/2022/04/2013_USAS_GTR.pdf (carries ISSF copyright marks).

## Considered, not listed

- Canada, Shooting Federation of Canada: https://www.sfc-ftc.ca/Rules . Its own rule book is for sporting and hunting rifle (2020, English and French); ISSF disciplines run on the ISSF rules. The Dominion of Canada Rifle Association rule book (2019) returned HTTP 403, so it is unverified.
- Australia: only snippets seen (Shooting Australia requires ISSF rules at supervised events; Target Pistol Australia publishes a 2026 addendum to the ISSF pistol rules at https://pistol.org.au/issf/). Not opened, so unverified. No rifle body checked.
- Denmark (Danish Shooting Union, http://www.skytteunion.dk), Belgium, Ireland, other members: not searched.
- Precision rifle (PRS), IHMSA, IBS, IMSSU silhouette (hit or miss steel, no scored faces): not read beyond titles.
